Email signature

A Texas A&M University–San Antonio tool that previews a branded email signature and copies it for use in Outlook.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Outlook

Project overview

The Texas A&M University–San Antonio brand guide includes a signature builder for Outlook. Faculty and staff can enter their own details in a form and see them in a shared layout with the university logo.

How the builder works

How the form handles variation

Contact details can vary across departments and roles. The form supports those differences within the same signature layout.

  • Multiple titles or contact details

    People can add another title, email, phone number, or website. The preview groups filled entries on the corresponding line and separates them with a vertical bar.

  • Optional contact information

    The generated signature omits the email, office phone, or website line when its main field is empty. A second address line is available for offices that need it.

The Outlook handoff

The copy action assembles a formatted HTML signature with inline styles and the university’s hosted logo image. This gives users a single block to paste into Outlook, including the contact details and branding they saw in the preview.
