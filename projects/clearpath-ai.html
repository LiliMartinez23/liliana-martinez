ClearPathAI

A study tool that generates two summaries from the same passage and checks the ADHD-friendly version against ten readability and structure rules.

  • Python
  • Streamlit
  • Generative AI

Project overview

ClearPathAI turns dense study material into notes organized for learners with ADHD. The Streamlit app produces a baseline summary and a structured version from the same passage. The structured one organizes the material into learning objectives, key concepts, and recall questions.

I led evaluation and documentation for the team. I recorded how the two versions scored on readability and structure, reran ten sample passages three times each, and documented file-upload issues I found while testing.

How the summary generator works

What the rules measure

The Python checker scores ten rules equally. A summary passes the project’s threshold when it meets at least eight of them.

  • Length and readability

    The checker looks for sentences of up to 20 words, paragraphs of up to 80 words, and a Flesch–Kincaid grade level of 8 or below.

  • Structure and recall

    It looks for three named sections, blank lines between them, three to six key-concept bullets, and at least two recall questions. Formulas have their own lines.

What the evaluation showed

Across ten passages and three runs per passage, the baseline summaries scored 20–40% on the project’s rules. The structured summaries scored 90–100%. In the first run of the Python textbook passage, the Flesch–Kincaid grade level fell from 10.9 to 4.5. These measures describe the text’s structure and readability.

I pasted the passages for every scored run to keep the input method consistent. In a separate upload check, TXT generated summaries successfully, while PDF and DOCX hit errors. I documented those failures for follow-up.
